Software Requirements Specification — QuickBite Food Delivery App (v0.3, draft)

1. Introduction
QuickBite connects customers with local restaurants and delivery partners in Tier-2 Indian cities. This document lists the requirements for the first release.

2. Functional Requirements

2.1 Accounts
- The system shall allow a customer to register using a mobile number and a one-time password (OTP).
- The OTP shall expire 5 minutes after it is sent.
- The system should let users log in quickly and easily.
- A customer shall be able to save up to 5 delivery addresses.
- It must support social login etc.

2.2 Browsing and ordering
- The system shall display restaurants within 7 km of the customer's selected address, sorted by estimated delivery time.
- Customers shall be able to filter restaurants by cuisine, rating and veg-only.
- The system shall allow customers to add items to a cart, apply a coupon and place an order, and shall send an SMS and email confirmation and update the restaurant dashboard.
- The cart may hold items from more than one restaurant.
- An order can only contain items from a single restaurant.
- Coupon rules are TBD.

2.3 Payments
- The system shall support UPI, debit/credit cards and cash on delivery.
- Payments shall be processed through an external payment gateway.
- If a payment fails, the system shall retain the cart and allow the customer to retry payment.
- Refunds should be processed as soon as possible.

2.4 Delivery
- The system shall assign each order to the nearest available delivery partner.
- Customers shall see the live location of the delivery partner on a map, updated every 10 seconds.
- Delivery partners shall be able to mark an order as picked up and delivered.

2.5 Restaurant dashboard
- Restaurant staff shall be able to accept or reject an incoming order within 3 minutes; unanswered orders shall be auto-rejected.
- Restaurant staff shall be able to mark menu items as out of stock.
- The admin shall be able to onboard new restaurants and deactivate restaurants.

3. Non-Functional Requirements
- The app should be fast.
- The home screen shall load within 2 seconds on a 4G connection for 95% of requests.
- The system shall support 5,000 concurrent users.
- The system must be secure and user-friendly.
- All payment data shall be handled in compliance with PCI DSS.
- The system shall be available 99.5% of the time each month, excluding planned maintenance.
- The app shall be usable in English and Hindi.
- The interface should be intuitive for elderly users.
- Customer data shall be retained as appropriate.
